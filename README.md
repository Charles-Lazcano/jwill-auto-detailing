# J.Will Auto Detailing

Marketing site for J.Will Auto Detailing — fully mobile auto detailing serving San Antonio, TX & surrounding areas, plus commercial fleet and food truck / kitchen hood cleaning.

Single-page site in `index.html` (no build step, no dependencies). Sections: hero, services & pricing, before/after sliders, commercial & fleet, maintenance plans, gallery, reviews, service area, and booking/contact.

## Editing

- **Prices & copy**: edit directly in `index.html` — pricing cards are under `#services`, plan pricing under `#plans`.
- **Photos**: the before/after sliders and gallery currently use illustrated line-art placeholders. Swap in real job photos by replacing the inline `<svg>` blocks with `<img>` tags.
- **Booking link**: currently points to `linktr.ee/J.Willautodetailing` — update every `href` with that URL, or swap in an embedded scheduler (Calendly/Square).
- **Lead forms**: the personal and commercial quote forms hand off to a `mailto:hello@jwillautodetailing.com` placeholder — update that address, or point the forms at a real form backend.
